=====================================================================
  TV HOME + DEBLOAT  -  make a slow, ad-filled TCL TV fast again
=====================================================================

WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
This kit runs on a laptop that's on the same Wi-Fi as your TV. It:

  1. Finds your TV on the Wi-Fi and connects to it.
  2. Backs up the TV's current setup to the laptop BEFORE changing anything.
  3. Switches off the junk: manufacturer ads, tracking ("viewing data"),
     sponsored rows and pop-ups. It ASKS YOU before every group, and it
     only switches things off - it never deletes or uninstalls anything.
  4. Installs "TV Home", a clean, fast home screen in the style of Apple TV:
     a row of your favourite apps over slowly changing nature pictures,
     with a clock. No ads, no recommendations, no tracking.
  5. Gives you a one-click UNDO that puts everything back exactly as it was.

After each step it takes a screenshot from the TV and asks whether
everything still looks right. If you say no, that step is undone on the spot.

The laptop only needs to stay on while it runs (about 20-30 minutes).
After that, everything lives on the TV and you won't need the laptop
unless you want to undo something or change the app row.

Works on TCL Google TV / Android TV sets (and most other Android TVs).
It does NOT work on Roku TVs (TCL also sells those - if your TV's home
screen says "Roku", this isn't for you).


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
  * A Windows laptop or PC on the same Wi-Fi as the TV
    (a Mac works too - see "MAC USERS" near the end)
  * The TV remote
  * About 30 minutes


=====================================================================
PART 1 - SET UP THE LAPTOP (one time, about 10 minutes)
=====================================================================

1. Install "Git for Windows". It provides the program that runs these
   scripts.
     - Go to  https://git-scm.com/download/win
     - Download it, then click "Next" through the installer.
       The default options are all fine.

2. Install "adb", the free Google tool that talks to the TV.
     - Press the Windows key, type  cmd  and open "Command Prompt"
     - Type this and press Enter:
           winget install Google.PlatformTools
     - When it says it's finished, close the window.
     - Restart the laptop (so Windows can find the new tool).

   (If "winget" isn't recognised: download "SDK Platform-Tools for
    Windows" from https://developer.android.com/tools/releases/platform-tools
    and unzip it so the "platform-tools" folder sits INSIDE this folder,
    next to this file.)

3. Unzip this kit somewhere easy, like your Desktop.
   (Right-click the zip > "Extract All" > "Extract".)


=====================================================================
PART 2 - SET UP THE TV (one time, about 3 minutes)
=====================================================================

4. Unlock the hidden "Developer options" menu:
     - On the TV go to  Settings > System > About
     - Scroll down to "Build" (it may say "Android TV OS build")
     - Press OK on it 7 times, until it says "You are now a developer"

5. Turn on debugging:
     - Go back to  Settings > System > Developer options
     - Turn on "USB debugging"
     - If you also see "Network debugging" or "Wireless debugging",
       turn that on too.
   (This is what lets the laptop talk to the TV over Wi-Fi.
    It's perfectly safe; you can turn it off again afterwards.)

6. Find the TV's IP address, just in case the laptop can't find the TV
   on its own:
     - Settings > Network & Internet > your Wi-Fi network
     - Write down the "IP address", e.g.  192.168.1.50


=====================================================================
PART 3 - RUN IT (about 15-20 minutes)
=====================================================================

7. In this folder, double-click   Run on Windows.bat
     - If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click
       "More info" > "Run anyway". (It appears because the file was
       downloaded; it isn't a virus warning about this file.)

   A black window opens and asks questions. In every question:
     * The capital letter is the suggested answer: [Y/n] means
       "yes unless you say otherwise". Just press ENTER to accept it.
     * Type  y  or  n  and press Enter to choose yourself.

8. CONNECT
     - It searches for the TV. If it finds it, confirm with Enter.
       If not, type the IP address from step 6 and press Enter.
     - >>> LOOK AT THE TV NOW <<<  A box "Allow USB debugging?" pops up.
       Tick "Always allow from this computer" and press OK.
       (If you miss it, just run the .bat file again.)

9. BACKUP - runs by itself.
     - If it asks "Also copy their APK files to this laptop?", press
       Enter (no - you don't need them).

10. CLEAN-UP (the "debloat")

    a) First it asks which TV features you use, e.g. voice search,
       casting from your phone, live TV / antenna, soundbar control.
       Say YES (or just press Enter) to anything you use or MIGHT use.
       Everything related to those is then left completely alone.

    b) Then it shows each group of junk, one at a time:
         - Advertising
         - Tracking / "viewing data"
         - Promotions and sponsored stuff
         - Feedback and survey pop-ups
       Press Enter to switch off the whole group, n to skip it, or r to
       go through it one item at a time.

    c) After EACH group, look at the TV. If it looks and works normally,
       press Enter. If anything seems broken, type  n  and that group is
       switched straight back on.

    d) Two lists where nothing is picked for you:
         - "TCL's own apps I couldn't classify"
         - "Other preinstalled apps"
       Type the numbers of any you never use (e.g.  1 3  or  2-4),
       or just press Enter to skip. When in doubt, skip.

    e) Speed tweaks: press Enter for both (faster menus, freed storage).

11. NEW HOME SCREEN
     - It installs TV Home and shows it on the TV. Move left and right
       with the remote. If it looks right, press Enter.
     - Press Enter again to make it your home screen.
     - If it says "The Home button still goes to the old home screen",
       that's normal on Google TV: Google keeps its own home screen in
       charge of the Home button. Type  y  to switch the Google home
       screen off. It's only switched off, not deleted (Undo brings it
       back), and the script checks it worked - if not, it puts
       everything back by itself.

12. When it says "All done", close the window.

13. Restart the TV: hold the remote's power button > Restart.

That's it. You can unplug / switch off the laptop.


=====================================================================
USING TV HOME
=====================================================================

  * Left / right moves along your apps. OK opens one.
  * The row starts with: Netflix, YouTube, Disney+, Peacock, Prime Video,
    Hulu, HBO Max, then "All Apps" and "Settings".
  * An app you haven't installed shows as a faded tile. Press OK on it
    to open its page in the Play Store and install it.
  * "All Apps" shows everything installed on the TV.
  * After 45 seconds without touching the remote, everything except the
    picture fades away, like a screensaver. Any button brings it back.
  * The pictures change every minute. They're drawn by the app itself
    (mountains, ocean sunset, northern lights, forest, desert, lake),
    so they're different every time and need no internet.

CHANGE WHICH APPS ARE IN THE ROW - on the TV, no laptop needed:
  * Add an app:     open All Apps, highlight the app, HOLD the OK button
                    (or press Menu) > "Add to dock"
  * Move / remove:  on the home screen, highlight the app, HOLD OK >
                    "Move left", "Move right" or "Remove from dock"

USE YOUR OWN PHOTOS AS THE BACKGROUND (needs the laptop):
  1. Copy .jpg or .png photos into the "wallpapers" folder in this kit
     (wide landscape photos look best).
  2. Double-click   Update dock and wallpapers (Windows).bat

CHANGE THE APP ROW OR SETTINGS FROM THE LAPTOP (optional):
  * Open  config\dock.txt  in Notepad. Each line is  Name|app id .
    You can reorder lines, delete lines, switch the clock to 24-hour
    (@clock|24h), change how often the picture changes
    (@wallpaper_seconds|60), or turn the slow picture drift off
    (@motion|off).
  * Then double-click   Update dock and wallpapers (Windows).bat


=====================================================================
UNDO EVERYTHING
=====================================================================

  1. Make sure the TV is on, on the same Wi-Fi, and the laptop is on.
  2. Double-click   Undo everything (Windows).bat
  3. Press Enter to confirm.
  4. Restart the TV.

This switches every app back on, puts the settings back, brings back the
original home screen, and removes TV Home.

IMPORTANT: keep the "tv-data" folder that appears inside this kit after
the first run. It holds the backup, and Undo needs it. Don't delete this
kit from the laptop until you're sure you're happy.


=====================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
=====================================================================

"Git for Windows is needed"
    -> Do step 1, then double-click the .bat file again.

"adb isn't installed"
    -> Restart the laptop after step 2. If it still says that, use the
       platform-tools download described in step 2.

It can't find or connect to the TV
    -> TV switched on (not standby)? Both on the same Wi-Fi network?
       "USB debugging" still on? Then run the .bat file again - it's
       always safe to run again; it picks up where it left off.
    -> Missed the "Allow USB debugging?" box on the TV? Run it again
       and watch the TV.

Something on the TV stopped working after the clean-up
    -> Double-click  Undo everything (Windows).bat
       (Or, to keep TV Home but find the culprit: run the clean-up again
        and say no to the group that caused it.)

The TV shows a black screen after pressing Home
    -> Don't panic, the laptop can still reach it. Double-click
       Undo everything (Windows).bat  and restart the TV.

Want to see the screenshots it took along the way?
    -> Open  tv-data  > backups > (the folder inside) > any folder named
       "screenshots..." and open  index.html .
       (A screenshot can come out black while a streaming app is
        playing - that's the app's copy protection, not a problem.)

The TV installed a software update and some ads came back
    -> Just double-click  Run on Windows.bat  again.


=====================================================================
COMMON QUESTIONS
=====================================================================

Is it safe?
    Nothing is deleted. Apps are only switched off, the same way you
    could switch them off yourself in Settings > Apps, and Undo switches
    them all back on. Core parts of the TV (Google Play, picture and
    sound, HDMI, remote pairing, system updates, voice search if you use
    it) are on a protected list and are never offered.

Will the TV still get updates?
    Yes. The update services are protected and never switched off.

Does TV Home spy on me or use the internet?
    No. It has no internet permission at all, no ads, no tracking. It's
    a tiny app (36 KB) that only shows your apps, a clock and pictures.

Do I need to keep the laptop connected?
    No. Only while the kit runs, or later to undo or update.

Should I turn off USB debugging afterwards?
    You can (Settings > System > Developer options). Turn it back on if
    you ever want to undo or update.

Some settings worth switching off by hand (the kit reminds you at the end):
    - Settings > Privacy > Usage & diagnostics: off
    - Settings > Privacy > Ads: delete advertising ID / opt out
    - TCL: Settings > Privacy (or System > Advanced) >
      "Viewing information services": off


=====================================================================
MAC USERS
=====================================================================

  * Skip Git for Windows.
  * Install adb: open Terminal and run
        brew install android-platform-tools
    (No Homebrew? Get it first from https://brew.sh )
  * Instead of double-clicking the .bat files, open Terminal, type  cd
    and a space, drag this folder onto the Terminal window, press Enter,
    then run:
        bash run_all.sh                      (instead of Run on Windows.bat)
        bash restore.sh                      (Undo everything)
        bash 4_launcher.sh --update-dock     (Update dock and wallpapers)
